## New in 1.4.0
**Frame interpolation (optional, for strong PCs)**
Right-click Mugetsu's title bar → **Frame interpolation**. Mugetsu then draws extra in-between pictures at your monitor's refresh rate, placing everything between where it was on the game's last two frames: characters, monsters, attacks, sliding panels, skill cooldowns and pulsing glows move smoothly. Every picture is really drawn, nothing is guessed, so text and the interface stay sharp (unlike frame generation tools). In busy rooms it picks the highest even rate your PC can keep up with. It's off by default: it shows each frame about one game frame later (about 33 ms at 30 FPS), and effects drawn frame by frame (many spell animations) still play at the game's own rate.

**Fixes for graphics cards with 4 GB or less**
Long sessions could fill up a smaller card's graphics memory (after a big boss fight, or after hours AFK in a busy town), which made the game drop to 2–3 FPS or players show up with parts of their gear missing. Mugetsu now uses its graphics memory much more efficiently, and when it gets close to the limit Windows gives it, it frees spare pictures and shapes it hasn't drawn for a while. Cards with plenty of memory are unaffected.
