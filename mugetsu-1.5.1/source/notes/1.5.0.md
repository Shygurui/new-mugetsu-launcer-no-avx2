## New in 1.5.0
**Flash-style edge smoothing (new default)**
Shapes now get smooth edges the way Flash Player made them: each edge is drawn with a soft half-pixel border, instead of drawing the whole picture several times over (MSAA). It looks practically the same as before on Medium and High, and costs far less on built-in and older graphics: on a laptop with Intel UHD 620 graphics, a crowded town went from about 11 FPS to 26–30 FPS on High. Low still smooths only thin lines. If you prefer the old smoothing, right-click the game → **Edge smoothing → MSAA**.

**New in-game menu**
Right-click anywhere on the game for Mugetsu's settings: **Frame interpolation**, **Edge smoothing**, **Graphics** (Automatic / Vulkan / DirectX 12), **Show FPS**, **Full screen** and **Open log folder**. Changes apply right away (Graphics restarts Mugetsu). The menu scales with the window. Graphics is still in the title bar menu too.

**Frame interpolation costs much less**
Between two game frames only the moving objects' positions change, so Mugetsu now draws each in-between picture from the last one it drew instead of going through the whole scene again. That part of drawing an in-between picture is several times cheaper, so busy rooms reach higher rates on high-refresh monitors. The pictures are the same as before.

**Smaller fixes**
- Effects with many copies of an animated shape at different sizes (torches, spell effects) no longer rebuild their shapes on every draw.
